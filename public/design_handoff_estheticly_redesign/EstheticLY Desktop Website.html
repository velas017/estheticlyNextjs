Before your visit

How to prepare.

I strongly encourage these tips before your appointment. Reach out anytime with questions.

01

Preparing for the facial

  • Discard retinol or acids for a week — no exfoliating 3 days prior.
  • Avoid waxing or tanning for 24 hours.
  • Try to avoid heavy makeup; I'll remove it if needed.
  • Remove any necklaces.
  • Get cozy! Robes are provided to your comfort level.
02

Can I still get a facial while using…?

  • Contraindications: tretinoin and accutane (stop use for 6 months).
  • No injections, botox, or laser for a month prior.
  • Reach out about prescribed dermatologist medication.
  • I work with inflamed acne, but cannot diagnose your skin.
After your visit

Make results last.

Follow these to ensure the best results post-treatment. I'll also walk through specifics during your visit.

3d

First 72 hours

  • No exfoliation for 3 days.
  • Wear SPF every single day.
  • Stay hydrated — water and sleep.
  • Avoid touching your face.
  • No sweating, heat, or sauna.
4–8

Cadence & maintenance

  • Recommended every 4–8 weeks as the skin cycle renews.
  • Consistency promotes cell turnover and collagen production.
  • Avoid makeup for at least 24 hours post-treatment.
  • Have a specific concern? We'll get there together, faster.
FAQ

Frequently asked.

Below you'll find resources related to facial care practices and answers to common questions.

What are the benefits of a facial?
Your skin is the largest organ on your body — invest in it. Facials improve skin health, even tone and texture, increase product absorption, and much more.
What product line will be used?
My back bar is a variety of brands: skinscript, glymed, dermalogica, circadia and more. Most are pharmaceutical lines or developed by estheticians, so I can cater to different clients and concerns.
What is the age requirement for a facial?
The minimum age allowed is 13. Teen facials are available with a guardian's consent. Facials are recommended for any age and gender.
I have sensitive skin. Can I get a facial?
Yes — facials are catered to all skin types. We'll use calmer, gentler products for sensitive skin.
Which facial should I book?
If this is your first facial, any customized facial is ideal. During the skin analysis, I'll determine your current condition, sensitivities, and underlying issues to ensure the best treatment for you.
